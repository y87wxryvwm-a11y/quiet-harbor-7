HOW TO WORK
The last run worked entirely in code. It fetched filings in code, ran,
found surprises, fetched again and reran, many times over, and it took
far too long. This time, use Amazon Bedrock agents for the
investigative work, and use code for building and checking.
1. Download once. Fetch each company's EDGAR filing history and each
   filing you need once, into a local cache. Every later step reads the
   cache. Nothing already cached is downloaded again.
2. Investigate before coding. Before writing the rules for a field,
   send Bedrock agents to read a small, varied set of real companies:
   a few of each type (traditional IPO, SPAC, de-SPAC, reverse merger,
   spin-off, uplisting, debt-only issuer, a company that went dark and
   came back). Each agent returns structured records (CIK, field,
   value, accession, exact quote) and a list of the edge cases it saw.
   Turn those into code rules, and keep the companies as test cases
   with their expected values.
3. Test on known cases first. Run each new or changed rule on its test
   cases and the companies it affects. Run the full population only
   when those pass.
4. Send leftovers to agents, not to more code loops. Rows the code
   can't settle (failed checks, unclear type) go to Bedrock agents in
   batches, with the relevant filing passages. Answers come back
   structured, with a quote; code then checks each quote is really in
   the filing. When several leftovers share a pattern, make it a code
   rule.
5. Keep a growing edge-case file (edge_cases.csv: CIK, field, issue,
   decision, accession, quote). The checks read it, so decisions stay
   fixed when you rebuild.
6. If a step takes more than about 10 minutes, find out why before
   running it again.

DEFINITIONS
- Effective date: the date the company became subject to SEC reporting
  requirements. For most companies (traditional IPOs) this is the first
  time its securities were sold to the public. By type:
  - Traditional IPO: the IPO registration statement's effective date
    (the SEC's EFFECT notice).
  - SPAC, and any company that came out of a de-SPAC: the effective
    date of the original SPAC's IPO, even if the registrant's CIK
    changed through a new holding company.
  - Reverse merger: the date the public company that acquired it
    became subject to reporting.
  - Uplisting: the first date the company became subject to SEC
    reporting, not the date it listed on a national exchange.
  - Spin-off: the effective date of the spun-off company's own
    registration statement (usually a Form 10).
  - Debt-only issuer: the effective date of its debt registration
    statement.
  - Went dark and came back: if the company stopped reporting (for
    example, filed a Form 15) and later resumed, use the date reporting
    resumed.
- Censoring: if the effective date is before 2015-01-01, record BEFORE
  2015 and don't pin down the exact date. A company is censored only if
  it filed a 10-K or 10-Q before 2015-01-01 and has not stopped
  reporting since.
- Traditional IPO: the company's first SEC-registered, underwritten
  sale of its common stock (or units) to the public for cash, with the
  shares listed on a US exchange. SPAC IPOs count. Not traditional:
  direct listing, reverse merger, spin-off, Form 10 registration, Reg A
  offering, uplisting of shares that already traded publicly (OTC or
  abroad), or debt-only registration.
- EGC self-report: what the company itself says in a filing. 1 if the
  cover checkbox is ticked or the text says it is an emerging growth
  company. 0 if the checkbox is unticked or the text says it is not.
  NONE if the filing has no checkbox and says nothing either way.
  (Registration statements, 10-Qs and 10-Ks have had the checkbox
  since 2017-04-12.)
- Revenue test: 1 if total revenue for the last full fiscal year before
  the date in question is below the EGC limit, otherwise 0. The limit
  is $1.0 billion before 2017-04-12, $1.07 billion from 2017-04-12, and
  $1.235 billion from 2022-09-20.
- Supplemented with the revenue test: use the self-report where it is 1
  or 0. Use the revenue test only where the self-report is NONE.

CELL VALUES (both files)
- NONE = truly none (for example, no de-SPAC happened, or no filing in
  the window states EGC status)
- NOT COLLECTED = depends on an effective date before 2015
- EXCLUDED = a row outside the research scope
- Blank = not yet known. None may remain at the end.
- Dates as YYYY-MM-DD. Money in US dollars as plain numbers.

FINAL FILE: reg_count_final_2025_ipo.csv
Every original row and column exactly as in the input (no deletions,
re-sorting or edits), plus these columns:

Becoming public
1. effective_date: as defined above, or BEFORE 2015.
2. going_public_type: traditional IPO, SPAC IPO, direct listing,
   reverse merger, spin-off, Form 10 (other), Reg A, uplisting,
   debt-only, or resumed reporting. There is no de-SPAC category: a
   company that came out of a de-SPAC is SPAC IPO.
3. ipo: 1 if going_public_type is traditional IPO or SPAC IPO, else 0.
4. spac: 1 if the first entity that became subject to reporting was a
   SPAC, whether it is still a SPAC or has since merged.
5. final_prospectus_url: direct link to the IPO's final prospectus
   document on sec.gov (the first 424B filing under the IPO
   registration's file number on or after the effective date). NONE if
   ipo = 0.
6. final_prospectus_date: its EDGAR filing date.

EGC status (NOT COLLECTED when censored)
7. egc_at_effective_self: EGC self-report from any of the company's
   filings within 10 days before or after the effective date. NONE if
   no filing in that window states it.
8. egc_at_effective: column 7 supplemented with the revenue test.
9. egc_first_report_self: EGC self-report from the first 10-Q or 10-K
   filed after the effective date.
10. egc_first_report: column 9 supplemented with the revenue test.

Reporting history (fill for every researched row, censored or not)
11. first_10k_current_string: the filing date of the first 10-K in the
    most recent unbroken run of fiscal-year 10-Ks ending with the 2025
    annual report. Use original 10-K filing dates, not amendments.
    Example: 10-Ks for every fiscal year 2019-2024 means the FY2019
    10-K's date. FY2019, then a gap, then FY2022-2024 means the FY2022
    date. A 10-KT (transition report after a fiscal year change) does
    not break the run.

After becoming public (fill for every researched row; for censored
rows, look only at events on or after 2015-01-01)
12. despac_date: if the company took part in a de-SPAC after its
    effective date, the date the merger closed. Otherwise NONE.
13. egc_after_despac_self: EGC self-report from the first filing after
    the de-SPAC date that states it.
14. revenue_after_despac: total revenue for the first reporting period
    ending after the de-SPAC date.
15. revenue_after_despac_period_end and
16. revenue_after_despac_months: that period's end date and length
    (3, 6, 9 or 12 months), so the revenue figure can be read.
17. reverse_merger_date: if the company took part in a reverse merger
    with a public acquirer that is not a SPAC after its effective date,
    the date of the most recent one. Otherwise NONE.
18-21. egc_after_reverse_merger_self, revenue_after_reverse_merger,
    revenue_after_reverse_merger_period_end,
    revenue_after_reverse_merger_months: same as 13-16, for the
    reverse merger.

Build this file in code from the working file. Never edit it by hand.

CHECKS (run in code; fix rows and rerun until the only failures left
are real, explained exceptions)
Structure
- rows_intact: the final file has the same rows, order and original
  values as the input, compared cell by cell.
- final_matches_working: the final file's answer columns equal the
  working file's, row by row.
- no_blanks: no blank new cell in a researched row.
- valid_values: 1/0 columns hold only 1, 0 or NONE where allowed; dates
  are valid YYYY-MM-DD; categories come from the lists above; accession
  numbers match EDGAR's format.
- consistent_fill: ipo = 0 means prospectus columns are NONE; censored
  rows have NOT COLLECTED in columns 2-10; EXCLUDED only on rows
  outside the scope; no despac_date means columns 13-16 are NONE, and
  the same for reverse mergers.
- unique_cik: each CIK appears once.
Sources
- quotes_verbatim: every quote is found word for word in the filing
  its accession points to.
- links_work: every URL returns HTTP 200 from sec.gov and opens the
  document itself, and the URL's accession equals the recorded one.
- same_company: every cited filing is filed by the row's CIK, or by a
  predecessor recorded in the working file.
Effective date
- effective_has_evidence: every effective date has the filing that made
  the company subject to reporting, and that filing's form fits the
  type (S-1/S-11 for IPOs, Form 10 for spin-offs, and so on).
- no_reports_before: the company filed no 10-K or 10-Q before its
  effective date, unless a Form 15 or a reporting gap comes between
  them (the went-dark case).
- censoring_right: BEFORE 2015 only where a 10-K or 10-Q was filed
  before 2015-01-01 and reporting never stopped afterwards.
- type_fits: ipo = 1 exactly when the type is traditional IPO or SPAC
  IPO; spac = 1 exactly when the type is SPAC IPO.
- spac_evidence: spac = 1 has a SIC code of 6770 or "blank check"
  language in the original IPO filing.
IPO
- same_registration: the prospectus is filed under the IPO
  registration's file number.
- prospectus_timing: final_prospectus_date is 0 to 5 days after the
  effective date.
- final_not_preliminary: the offer price is a single number, not a
  range.
EGC
- egc_window: the filing behind column 7 is dated within 10 days of the
  effective date.
- egc_quote_fits: every sentence-based EGC quote contains "emerging
  growth company".
- checkbox_era: every EGC self-report from a filing dated on or after
  2017-04-12 comes from its checkbox.
- supplement_rule: column 8 equals column 7 wherever 7 is 1 or 0, and
  column 10 equals column 9 wherever 9 is 1 or 0. Revenue-filled cells
  have the revenue, fiscal year end and limit recorded, and the 1 or 0
  matches them.
- first_report_is_first: the filing behind column 9 is the first 10-Q
  or 10-K filed after the effective date.
- egc_needs_new_equity: an EGC = 1 company had no registered sale of
  common stock before 2011-12-08.
- egc_changes_explained: where column 7 is 1 and column 9 is 0, the
  reason is recorded.
- revenue_matches_xbrl: every revenue figure equals the company's own
  XBRL value for the same period, where one exists.
10-K run
- run_unbroken: the recorded fiscal year ends run one year apart (or
  are bridged by a 10-KT) up to the 2025 annual report, all original
  10-Ks.
- run_starts_after_gap: the 10-K before the run's first one (if any)
  is more than one fiscal year earlier.
After becoming public
- events_after_effective: despac_date and reverse_merger_date are after
  the effective date (or on or after 2015-01-01 for censored rows).
- despac_has_closing: every despac_date matches the closing 8-K (Item
  2.01) and the merger partner's name is recorded.
- after_event_order: the EGC filing and revenue period behind columns
  13-16 and 18-21 are the first ones after the event.

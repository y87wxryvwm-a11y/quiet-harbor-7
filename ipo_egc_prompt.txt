This is a new project.

TASK
I'm studying how US companies go public and whether they were emerging
growth companies (EGCs) at their IPO. The base population is
reg_count_final_2025.csv. Each row is one company, identified by CIK
(column CIK).

You will build two files:
- A WORKING file with the answers plus the evidence and checks behind
  them. Your work is finished only when the working file passes every
  check below.
- A FINAL file: the original spreadsheet plus five answer columns,
  copied from the working file. This is the clean file I use in my
  research.

WHICH ROWS TO RESEARCH
Research a row only if column "form" is 10-K or 10-K/A AND column ABS
does not mark it as asset-backed. List ABS's distinct values in the
report and say which you treated as asset-backed. Put EXCLUDED in every
new column for all other rows.

Use only SEC EDGAR filings as sources.

DEFINITIONS
- Traditional IPO: the company's first SEC-registered, underwritten
  sale of its common stock (or units) to the public for cash, with the
  shares listed on a US exchange. NOT a traditional IPO: direct listing,
  merger with a SPAC (de-SPAC), reverse merger, spin-off, Form 10
  registration, Reg A offering, or an offering of shares that already
  traded publicly (OTC or abroad). If the company is itself a SPAC, its
  own SPAC IPO counts.
- Cutoff: full research only for IPOs whose registration became
  effective on or after 2015-01-01.
- EGC revenue limit by IPO effective date: $1.0 billion before
  2017-04-12; $1.07 billion from 2017-04-12; $1.235 billion from
  2022-09-20.

CELL VALUES (both files)
- NONE = truly none (for example, every IPO column when ipo = 0)
- NOT COLLECTED = an IPO effective before 2015-01-01 (fill only ipo
  and ipo_effective_date)
- EXCLUDED = a row outside the research scope
- Blank = not yet known. None may remain at the end.
- Dates as YYYY-MM-DD. Money in US dollars as plain numbers.

FINAL FILE: reg_count_final_2025_ipo.csv
Every original row and column exactly as in the input (no deletions,
re-sorting or edits), plus these five columns:
1. ipo: 1 or 0.
2. ipo_effective_date: the date of the SEC's EFFECT notice for the IPO
   registration statement.
3. final_prospectus_url: a direct link to the prospectus document on
   sec.gov. It is the first 424B filing under the IPO registration
   statement's file number on or after the effective date.
4. final_prospectus_date: its EDGAR filing date.
5. egc_at_ipo: 1 or 0.
Build this file in code from the working file. Never edit it by hand.

WORKING FILE: reg_count_final_2025_ipo_working.xlsx
Sheet "data": CIK, the five answer columns, then these evidence
columns:

IPO evidence
- ipo_route: if ipo = 0, how the company became public: direct
  listing, de-SPAC, reverse merger, spin-off, Form 10, Reg A,
  uplisting, or never sold stock publicly. IPO if ipo = 1.
- ipo_route_accession: the EDGAR filing that shows the route.
- ipo_route_quote: an exact quote from that filing (under 40 words)
  that shows the route.
- ipo_registration_form (S-1, S-11, etc.), ipo_registration_accession,
  ipo_file_number (333-xxxxxx), ipo_effect_notice_accession.

Final prospectus evidence (IPOs effective 2015 on)
- final_prospectus_accession, final_prospectus_form (424B4, 424B1,
  etc.)
- ipo_offer_price, ipo_exchange, ipo_lead_underwriter: from the
  prospectus cover.
- ipo_no_public_market_quote: the prospectus sentence saying there was
  no public market before the offering.

EGC evidence (IPOs effective 2015 on)
- egc_evidence_accession: the filing used (registration statement or
  final prospectus).
- egc_evidence_type: "cover checkbox" or "prospectus statement".
- egc_checkbox: checked, unchecked, or no box.
- egc_quote: an exact quote stating the company is (or is not) an EGC.
- pre_ipo_fiscal_year_end, pre_ipo_revenue: the last full fiscal year
  before the IPO and its total revenue, from the prospectus.
- egc_revenue_limit: the limit in force on the effective date.
- not_egc_reason: if egc_at_ipo = 0, one of: revenue over limit,
  nonconvertible debt over $1 billion, earlier registered sale of
  common stock before 2011-12-08, large accelerated filer, or company
  chose not to be an EGC.
- not_egc_evidence: the number, date or quote that proves the reason.
- first_10k_after_ipo_accession, first_10k_egc_checkbox.

Check results
- checks_failed: names of the checks this row fails, separated by
  semicolons. Empty if all pass.
- check_explanation: for each failed check, the filing's own words or
  numbers showing why the exception is real.

Sheet "checks": one row per check with its name, what it tests in plain
English, and how many rows passed and failed.

CHECKS (run in code; fix rows and rerun until the only failures left
are real, explained exceptions)
Structure
- rows_intact: the final file has the same rows, order and original
  values as the input, compared cell by cell.
- final_matches_working: the final file's five answer columns equal the
  working file's, row by row.
- no_blanks: no blank new cell in a researched row.
- valid_values: 1/0 columns hold only 1 or 0; dates are valid
  YYYY-MM-DD; categories come from the lists above; accession numbers
  match EDGAR's format.
- consistent_fill: ipo = 0 means IPO columns are NONE; a pre-2015 IPO
  means prospectus and EGC columns are NOT COLLECTED; EXCLUDED only on
  rows outside the scope.
- unique_cik: each CIK appears once.
Sources
- quotes_verbatim: every quote is found word for word in the filing
  its accession points to (fetch the filing and search it).
- links_work: every URL returns HTTP 200 from sec.gov and opens the
  document itself, and the URL's accession equals
  final_prospectus_accession.
- same_company: every cited filing is filed by the row's CIK (or a
  predecessor named in check_explanation).
IPO logic
- same_registration: the prospectus is filed under ipo_file_number.
- prospectus_timing: final_prospectus_date is 0 to 5 days after
  ipo_effective_date.
- final_not_preliminary: ipo_offer_price is a single number, not a
  range.
- ipo_before_10k: ipo_effective_date is before the first 10-K and
  before the 2025 annual report.
- registration_form_fits: the registration form is an IPO form (S-1,
  S-11, or their amendments).
- no_market_quote_present: every ipo = 1 from 2015 on has
  ipo_no_public_market_quote.
EGC logic
- egc_revenue: egc_at_ipo = 1 means pre_ipo_revenue is below
  egc_revenue_limit.
- egc_quote_fits: egc_quote contains "emerging growth company".
- checkbox_matches: for registrations filed on or after 2017-04-12,
  egc_checkbox agrees with egc_at_ipo.
- not_egc_has_reason: egc_at_ipo = 0 always has not_egc_reason and
  not_egc_evidence.
- first_10k_agrees: egc_at_ipo agrees with first_10k_egc_checkbox, or
  the difference is explained (for example, the company lost EGC
  status within its first year).
